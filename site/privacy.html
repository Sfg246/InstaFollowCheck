Privacy

FollowCheck never asks visitors for their Instagram password or session cookie.

When you scan a public username, that username is sent to the FollowCheck backend. The v3 backend makes anonymous requests to Instagram public-web surfaces. It is not configured with an Instagram account, password, sessionid, or paid data-provider API key.

The backend keeps a shared persistent cache of public profile and relationship pages so repeated scans can reuse recently fetched data. Cached relationship data may include Instagram numeric user IDs, usernames, display names, public/private flags, verification flags, and profile image URLs returned by the public-web response.

The comparison and selection interface run in your browser. FollowCheck stores lightweight preferences on your device, including the optional site access code, cooldown/ETA state, and usernames you mark as handled.

Normal server, tunnel, or reverse-proxy logs may contain request metadata such as IP address and timestamps. The app does not automate follows or unfollows.

Private Instagram targets are intentionally refused. Instagram may change, restrict, or rate-limit public-web access at any time. FollowCheck stops on access blocks or rate limits rather than trying to bypass them.

FollowCheck is not affiliated with Instagram or Meta.
